# Serve

Expose scored, classified content so research AI agents can search, filter, and return the most relevant results.

## What agents can do

- Filter content by topic, industry, or specialty
- Summarize and extract key insights on a subject
- Surface trends and hot topics by month or year
- Track price movements and market signals
- Answer research questions that require synthesizing information across many sources

## Retrieval behavior

Results favor items that passed Score (high quality) and respect topic assignments from Classify, ensuring agents always work from a clean, relevant knowledge base.
